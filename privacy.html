Privacy

Last updated October 18, 2026

The short version

  • We store what you put into the app so it can plan and track your workouts, and so your crew can train with you.
  • No ads. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers, and we don't track you on other websites.
  • We count how the app gets used, anonymously: no names, emails or workout details.
  • You can delete your account and your data any time: Settings → Delete my account.

What we store

  • Your sign-in: your name and email from Google or the email you sign up with. Passwords are handled by Google's sign-in service; we never see them.
  • Your plan answers: goals, experience, how many days you train, session length, home gear, and any injuries or limits you choose to tell us about, so the plan can work around them.
  • Your training: workouts, exercises, weights, reps, how each set felt, cardio, stretches, check-ins (including pain ratings if you add them) and notes.
  • Body measurements you choose to log, such as weight or waist.
  • Your body shape for the muscle map, if you set one (chest, shoulders, waist, hips and build). It's saved to your own account record, which only you can read. Your crew never sees it.
  • Your crew: which crew you're in, its name and invite code.
  • Feedback you send, with your name, so we can follow up.

Who can see it

  • You, on any phone you sign in on.
  • Your crew. A crew is a private group you join with an invite code. Everyone in it can access the crew's training records; that's how training together and partner logging work. The What you share setting on the Crew tab controls what the Crew tab shows the others: everything, just that you trained, or nothing. Only join crews with people you trust.
  • The people who run GymBunnies.fit can technically access the database to keep the app working and fix problems. We don't look through anyone's data otherwise. Feedback you send is read by the app's admin.

Anonymous usage counts

To see what's working, the app counts things like: which days it was opened, how many workouts and sets were logged, which screens and buttons get used, minutes with the app open, and a summary of plan choices (days a week, goals, experience, layout). Injuries and limits are not included.

These counts are tied to a scrambled, one-way code instead of your name or email, and never include what you lifted or wrote. The totals are readable by anyone who looks, because they contain nothing personal.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase stores your data and handles sign-in, on Google's servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app. Like any website host, GitHub may log basic visit information such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts supplies some of the typefaces, so your browser fetches font files from Google.
  • YouTube only if you tap a "See it done" link, which opens YouTube.

We don't use advertising networks, third-party analytics or tracking pixels.

On your phone

The app keeps a copy of your crew's data on your phone so it works with spotty gym signal. Signing out erases that copy.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete my account. This permanently erases your workouts, body measurements, check-ins, profile and feedback, takes you out of your crew, and deletes your sign-in. If you own the crew, it passes to the member who's been in it longest; if you're the last one, the whole crew is deleted. The anonymous usage counts stay, since nothing in them points back to you.

Want a copy first? Use Settings → Backup → Download backup.

Health information

GymBunnies.fit is a training log and planner, not a medical service, and nothing in it is medical advice. If you have an injury or condition, check with a doctor or physical therapist before starting a new routine.

Children

GymBunnies.fit isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions or requests? Send them from Settings → Feedback and ideas in the app.
